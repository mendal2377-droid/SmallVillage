# Coast and toy fireworks

The **Coast & beach** pin enters the full village at its new eastern shore. A continuous trail joins the forest at (300,128) to the sand, with a path along the dunes. The coast appears on both the 3D plan and the minimap. It is a fictional destination inspired by the supplied [Point Lookout reference](https://claude-creative-xbmc.vercel.app/), with original procedural geometry and shaders: turquoise shallows, deep blue sea, travelling white breakers, wet sand, a rocky headland, wind-bent painted trees, driftwood and gulls. No reference assets or source code are redistributed.

`coast-layout.js` shares the curved water boundary between the walker and planting mask. Freshwater ponds retain their separate fish and vegetation. Deep sea blocks walking and landing; the flight chicken remains available. The existing house and Blender exports are unchanged.

Select **5 / Firework Gatling**, then hold **P**, right mouse or the visible **Hold fireworks** button. Six rounded pastel barrels spin, launching a repeating sequence of coloured stars with soft whistles and pops. Release, pause, change tools or enter an activity to stop firing. The launcher is available throughout the day and in every season. Its fireworks are decorative and do not hit animals or knock down targets. Ten reusable rockets and 960 reusable spark slots bound the effects during sustained play.

Surf sound fades in near the shore and respects the existing sound switch. Water brightness, haze and storm swell follow the shared environment. The existing illustrated rendering applies to the new coast and toy.

Validation: production build; dry trail and curved sea boundary; 100 seconds of sustained pooled fire; release/pause/clear; existing tool input, targets/vehicles, stairs, forest and comfort controllers; real Edge desktop and phone map entry, keyboard/touch firing, day/night rendering and return to the house. Browser checks collect JavaScript and shader errors. Evidence lives in ignored `artifacts/coast/`.
