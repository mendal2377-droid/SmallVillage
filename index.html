01 / OBSERVED

The things we can see.

The tiled kitchen stove, red-cushioned sofas, bedroom furniture, red stairs and enclosed upstairs corridor follow the recordings, with temporary clutter cleared away.

02 / INTERPRETED

The space in between.

Building dimensions, hidden partitions, upstairs room furnishings and the surrounding village layout are estimates. This is a visual reconstruction, not a measured survey.
