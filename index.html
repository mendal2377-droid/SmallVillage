THE EVERYDAY, PRESERVED

Small details.
A sense of belonging.

Your home and roof videos, house and field photographs, a satellite reference and your drawn plan guide this reconstruction, from the rooms to the long roads through the fields.

01 / OBSERVED

The things we can see.

The tiled kitchen stove, red-cushioned sofas, bedroom furniture, red stairs and enclosed upstairs corridor follow the recordings, with temporary clutter cleared away.

02 / INTERPRETED

The space in between.

Village roads, waterways, ponds and the school follow your sketch and satellite reference. Building dimensions, individual plots and hidden room layouts remain estimated. This is a visual reconstruction, not a measured survey.

03 / OPEN TO EXPLORE

A scene you can make your own.

Walk through the rooms, climb both stair flights and look across the courtyard from the second floor. Download the editable Blender scene to continue building.
